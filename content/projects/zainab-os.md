# zainabOS

Most personal websites answer one question: *What have I done?*

Mine answers a different one: *Who am I when I'm creating?*

Visitors shouldn't feel like they're browsing pages. They should feel like they accidentally stepped inside my room while I was away. Not to invade my privacy. But to understand how I think.
