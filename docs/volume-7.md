# Chapter 1

# Philosophy

Most portfolios answer one question.

> What have you built?

This world answers six.

> What were you thinking?

> What were you reading?

> What were you struggling with?

> What changed your mind?

> What did you abandon?

> Why did you keep going?

Knowledge is not the finished artifact.

Knowledge is everything surrounding it.

# Chapter 20

# Exportability

Everything in the museum should be portable.

Every artifact should exist as structured data.

Example:

```yaml
title: CareerPilot
type: project

created: 2026-05-18

related:
  - notebook/idea-042
  - prompt/agent-design-v3
  - article/building-careerpilot
  - research/ats-analysis

status: active

tags:
  - AI
  - Careers
  - React
```

The website is merely one way of viewing the collection.

The knowledge itself should outlive the interface.

# Chapter 21

# AI as Archivist

AI is never the author.

AI is the archivist.

Its responsibilities are:

* Suggest connections between artifacts.
* Generate summaries.
* Surface forgotten ideas.
* Recommend related work.
* Build timelines.
* Detect recurring themes.
* Highlight contradictions in thinking.
* Find patterns across years of notes.

It organizes the museum.

It does not replace the curator.

---

# Chapter 22

# The Legacy Principle

Imagine the website is discovered twenty years from now.

Not by a recruiter.

Not by a client.

By someone who wants to understand how one person's mind worked.

Could they reconstruct:

* What fascinated you?
* What frightened you?
* What problems you kept returning to?
* How your ideas matured?
* Which beliefs changed?
* Which ones endured?

If yes, the museum has succeeded.

If all they find is a polished list of projects, it has failed.

## Appendix A

## Universal Artifact Schema

Every artifact, regardless of type, must implement the same structure:

```yaml
id:
title:
type:

created:
updated:

summary:

story:

origin:

related_artifacts:

related_people:

related_books:

related_projects:

related_prompts:

media:

status:

timeline:

reflection:

next_steps:

visibility:

tags:
```

This consistency means a journal entry can relate to a research paper, a prompt can point to a project, and a photograph can connect to a conference talk. Everything speaks the same language beneath the surface.

# Final Commandment

> **Don't build a portfolio.**
>
> **Don't build a blog.**
>
> **Don't build a second brain.**
>
> Build a **knowledge organism**.
>
> Every note feeds a project.
>
> Every project feeds an article.
>
> Every article changes a belief.
>
> Every belief shapes the next project.
>
> Years later, the visitor shouldn't remember the website's animations or shaders.
>
> They should remember that they spent two hours wandering through the evolution of a person's mind and somehow never felt lost.
