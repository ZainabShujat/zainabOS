I LOVE where this is going.

But I think you're about to make one mistake that almost every game makes.

You're treating onboarding as **instructions**.

I think zainabOS needs onboarding as an **experience**.

Those are very different.

---

# Think about Portal.

Portal never says:

> Press W to move.

Then another room.

Then another.

You learn naturally.

## One more thing...

I actually think you're missing one chapter in the House Bible.

# Visitor States

Every visitor moves through these emotional states.

```
Wonder

↓

Curiosity

↓

Orientation

↓

Exploration

↓

Discovery

↓

Connection

↓

Reflection

↓

Departure

↓

Return
```

Every architectural decision should support one of those states.

For example:

* The fly-through creates **Wonder**.
* The orientation teaches **Confidence**.
* The Atrium creates **Orientation**.
* The rooms create **Discovery**.
* The Observatory creates **Perspective**.
* The Therapy Room creates **Reflection**.
* The exit leaves **Curiosity to return**.

---

One tiny thing I'd change in your architecture document too.

Right now it starts with coordinates, floors, Y-values, implementation phases...

That's perfect for Antigravity.

But I'd create a document that comes *before* it.

**Volume XVII: The First Visit.**

Not a technical specification.

A screenplay.

Literally second-by-second.

```
0:00 Black.

0:03 Stars emerge.

0:08 A warm light appears.

0:15 The floating island comes into view.

0:25 "Welcome."

0:35 Camera circles the mansion.

0:50 Choose your exploration style.

1:10 Enter the Atrium.

1:20 Learn to move.

1:40 Open your first door.

2:00 The mansion is yours.
```

Because if you nail those first two minutes, everything that follows feels intentional. Visitors won't feel like they've opened a website. They'll feel like they've been invited into someone's world. And that's a memory people tend to carry with them. 🌌🏡
